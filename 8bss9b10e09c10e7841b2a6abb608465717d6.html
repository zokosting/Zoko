Axiom 1: Some roads should be fast
The aim of transport is connecting people with destinations. They can connect with more destinations if they can do so in less time. Ceteris paribus, faster roads will take less time. Without loss of generality, let’s call these roads highways.

Axiom 2: Some roads should be slow
Some roads serve neighborhoods and have traffic that is not just motor vehicles. Let’s call these roads streets.

Axiom 3: Fast roads (highways) attract traffic from slow roads (streets)
People, who are soft and move slowly, do not mix with vehicles, which are hard, when they move fast.“Stroads”—destroying their street function.
More traffic slows speeds –> slower speed lowers demand –> lessdemandreduces traffic.
His does not justify “upgrading” the street through widening, which takes out the very elements that made it successful in the first place.
