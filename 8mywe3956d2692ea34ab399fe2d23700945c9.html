Overtime over time, your child may have learned that you only really mean it when you are mad. It is a pattern.

The cycle is:
  • You ask nicely.
  • No response
  • You ask again.
  • Still nothing.
  • You yell.
  • They finally move.
What is the message learned? I don't have to act until the angry voice shows up. It's not disobedience, it's conditioning.
 Their brain begins to associate action with urgency, meaning anger.
 They are not just ignoring you. They have just learned to respond to tone, not words, not to you.
