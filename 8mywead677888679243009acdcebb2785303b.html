It means "I'm doing very well".
It's typically used in a sarcastic sense.

A: Are you okay?
B: yes, I'm just peachy! (when really person B is mad and being sarcastic)

It's used to kind say that the person asked a dumb question about your mood.
Or it can be used to show you really are ok...depends on your tone when you say it.
You could say "I'm just peachy" when with your tone you're indicating "of course I'm not.
