Aldo van Eyck’s playgrounds.
After the second world war this Dutch Archtiect designed more than 700 playgrounds for the city of Amsterdam which transformed numerous open and often derelict city spaces. These playground consisted of bare, geometrical shapes functioning as sandpits and climbing frames.
