All share some common qualities, including being very active almost any time of day and in any weather, populated by what appear to be locals as well as visitors, and all have third places fronting at least one side of the plaza.

Mixing the living room sort of amenities with the retail providers that people need regularly ends up naturally drawing people to linger for a wide variety of reasons.

This plaza lacks many of the things we think are key to successful public places like enclosure and active edges.
