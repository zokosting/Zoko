You're supposed to use every unfair advantage you have. Looks, genetics, connections, dad's money, whatever. There's nothing noble about choosing the hardest path just to feel like an underdog.

I remember this from Fresh Prince of Bel-Air. Uncle Phil called Will Smith's character dumb because he didn't want any doors opened for him.
"Nobody does anything without help, Will. People open doors for me and I work hard to open doors for you. It doesn't make you any less of a man to walk through them." - Uncle Phil

My mom always says “on your deathbed, no one is going to present you an award for living your life without help”.

Just make the world better.
